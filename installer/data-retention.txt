Your sound library catalog, metadata and preferences are retained.

SFX Vault stores its catalog.sqlite3 database, settings, metadata and update backups
under %LOCALAPPDATA%\SFX Vault, outside the installation directory.
The separate Update Test edition uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\SFX Vault Update Test.

Installing, upgrading or uninstalling the application does not delete these folders.
Source audio remains in its original locations and is never managed by the installer.
To remove user data later, back it up and explicitly remove the data folder yourself.
